# About This Log

{% shipAlert "info", "This transmission originates from deep space. Signal latency: 4.2 years." %}

**Stellar Log** is a personal transmission journal of Dr. MMira Vasquez, astrophysicist and sole crew member aboard the Interstellar Science Vessel *Perihelion*.

## The Mission

Launched in 2380 under the auspices of the Terran Exploration Consortium, *Perihelion*'s seven-year mission is to conduct close-range survey operations of systems within the 5 light-year radius beyond the Heliopause — the boundary where the Sun's solar wind yields to interstellar space.

Mira's role: observe, measure, document, and transmit. Everything she discovers, Earth won't know about for over four years.

## The Explorer

Dr. Vasquez holds degrees in astrophysics and astrobiology from the University of São Paulo and the Orbital Institute of Sciences. She was selected from over 12,000 applicants for the program, citing her ability to "tolerate extended isolation with grace." She would tell you that's debatable.

Before departure she spent three years in analog mission training in the Arctic and at the Martian South Pole base. Neither prepared her for the specific quality of silence at 4 light-years from home.

## The Ship

*Perihelion* is a 340-meter autonomous research vessel with an AI flight management system called CASS (Crew Autonomy Support System). CASS doesn't talk — it chirps status codes and adjusts cabin temperature without being asked. Mira has mixed feelings about this.

## Why Public Logs?

The Consortium requires all mission personnel to maintain public transmission logs. The stated reason is scientific transparency. Mira suspects it's also to remind her that someone, eventually, will read these.
