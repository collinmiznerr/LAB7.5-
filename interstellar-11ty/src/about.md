# About this log

This is an archive of my field notes—written for no one and for anyone.
I’m the last human I can verify, traveling alone through systems that don’t appear on any map I was given.

{% signal "Protocol" , "Each entry is written in plain language, time-stamped, and saved twice: once locally, once to a narrowbeam burst whenever I have line-of-sight to a relay." %}

## What you’ll find here

- **Daily observations** (light, dust, radio noise, ship health)
- **Encounters** with artifacts, signals, and the strange courtesy of alien physics
- **A record of feeling**, because a mind needs witnesses—even imagined ones

## Why the pine patch?

The patch is a quiet joke from training: *bring something that grows.*
A pine isn’t made for vacuum, but it’s made for persistence.

> If you’re reading this on a world with air, take a full breath for both of us.
